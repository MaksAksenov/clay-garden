# Stage 12. Store preparation: About / Licenses and listing screenshots — defense of the technical decisions

The user's task: "Yes, both" — to the proposal to fix what the `apk-factory/check_play.py` check found:
1. the game has no license notice: Godot is MIT, and its text and the notices of third-party components must ship with
   the game (a license requirement rather than a Play rule, but publishing without it is not an option);
2. the phone screenshots of 1600×720 (20:9, 2.22) fail the Play requirement "the long side is no more than twice the
   short one"; the listing needs 16:9 screenshots.

What exists now:
- the settings are an overlay (`ui.gd::_build_settings`, on a phone `ui_phone.gd::_build_settings`, two columns); at
  the bottom "Reset progress…" and "Done"; on top of them — the reset window; `close_top` closes the top window (Esc,
  "Back");
- the version exists only in the export preset (`version/name="0.9"`), the project's `config/version` is empty;
- `Engine.get_license_text()` — 1,149 characters (Godot's MIT); `Engine.get_copyright_info()` — 102 components;
  `Engine.get_license_info()` — 19 licenses, 77,412 characters;
- screenshots: `--shot=path.png` (a screenshot and exit), scenes by flags (`--level=N`, `--sandbox --fresh --seed=`,
  `--aim=x,y`, `--autosolve`, `--settings`, `--lang=`, `--touch`); trial 1920×1080 shots with `--touch` and the Mobile
  renderer — `shots/store_probe/` (the phone layout holds on a 1333×750 canvas; in the garden a one-time coach tip hangs
  over the island).

## Decisions

**D1. The About screen is a separate overlay**, entered by an "About" button in the bottom row of the settings
(between "Reset progress…" and "Done"), on PC and on a phone. Closed by "Done", Esc and "Back" — returns to the
settings (`close_top`: About first, then the settings).

**D2. Content:**
- the game's name (by language), the version, "Made with Godot Engine";
- "Music, sounds, models and the icon are generated by the game's code" — true (there are no third-party assets) and
  it answers "whose images are these";
- the Godot license text (`get_license_text`);
- "Third-party components": 102 lines "name — license" from `get_copyright_info`, with the copyright holders;
- the full texts of 19 licenses from `get_license_info`.

Legal texts are not translated (the English original); headings and captions follow the game's language.

**D3. One source for the version.** `config/version="0.9"` in `project.godot`; the preset's `version/name` is empty —
the export takes the project's version. The screen and the APK show the same thing; `check_play` compares
`versionName` with the project.

**D4. Speed.** 77k characters in one label on a phone — a noticeable pause (text shaping). The screen is built lazily
on first opening; the full license texts are hidden behind a "Full license texts" button and built only on it, in
chunks per license. A build-time measurement in a test (headless) — with a threshold.

**D5. Scrolling.** All content is in a `ScrollContainer` filling the panel; on touch — a finger (`scroll_deadzone` as
in the dock); the "Done" button is outside the scroll, always visible.

**D6. Listing shots — a script `tools/store_shots.py`.** It runs the game once per subject: 1920×1080 (16:9), the
Mobile renderer, `--touch` (the phone layout — what a player will see), both languages →
`store/<lang>/screenshots/NN_<subject>.png`. Subjects (6):
1. a summer garden (a finished pretty island, a seed);
2. a spring level with a try and the rules card (how coziness is counted);
3. a ★★★ level result;
4. winter (All Year Round with the season row);
5. autumn with falling leaves;
6. the season menu.

A new flag `--nocoach` removes the one-time coach tips from the frame.

**D7. 16:9 is an honest render, not a crop of 20:9.** The phone layout stretches (`expand`) to a 1333×750 canvas; the
layout test (`test_touch`) gets a 16:9 size. The alternative — 2:1 (2160×1080, closer to the Pixel) — passes Play's
limit, but the listing and Play's recommendations for games are oriented to 16:9.

**D8. `check_play` looks at screenshots per language** (`store/<lang>/screenshots/`) — each language has its own
listing.

**D9. Acceptance checks:**
- `test_settings`: About opens from the settings, closes with "Done", Esc and "Back" (to the settings, not the menu);
  the version on the screen = `config/version`; the full texts are built only on the button; the build time is under a
  threshold;
- `test_touch`: the About layout at 1333×600, 1200×600 and 1333×750 in both languages;
- listing shots: `check_play --store` without FAILs on screenshots;
- the APK is rebuilt: `versionName` = `config/version`, `check_play` in debug mode without FAILs and without the
  license WARN.

## Defense result

The skeptic's main finding: **the set of notices is incomplete.** `get_copyright_info` / `get_license_info` /
`get_license_text` cover only Godot and its `thirdparty/` (C++). The dex of our APK contains AndroidX (17 packages:
activity, core, fragment, lifecycle, profileinstaller…), the Kotlin standard library and kotlinx.coroutines, a piece
of Guava (ListenableFuture), JSpecify, JetBrains annotations, Animal Sniffer — all Apache-2.0 / MIT, and Godot doesn't
list them (checked in the dex: `Landroidx/` 3,935 references, `Lkotlin/` 3,555, `Lkotlinx/coroutines` 2,379). A manual
"Android" block is needed. The second finding: **`store/` would have leaked into the APK** (`all_resources` mode
imports PNGs). The third: **a ScrollContainer inside a CenterContainer shrinks to zero**, and a wrapping label without
horizontal scrolling disabled wraps letter by letter.

The player's main finding: **the shots sell the wrong things.** The first two frames must say "cozy" and "this is a
puzzle"; an empty level with a "New item" toast and a wall of rules doesn't sell; the season menu is a grid of locks
and "38 of 300" — that says "lots of work", not coziness. Accepting the listing is done by eye on thumbnails, not by
file size.

## Stage 12 results

**Done.**
- **About** — a child node of the settings (`ui.gd::_build_about`, entry: on PC — the bottom row left of "Done", on a
  phone — the right column under the flags). On top: the name, the version (`config/version`, the single source — the
  preset takes it, `version/name` is empty), "Made with Godot Engine", "everything is generated by the game's code";
  below — the Godot license; on a button — the 102 Godot components with their copyright holders, an "Android
  libraries" block (`ANDROID_LIBS`) and the texts of 19 licenses (original). A full-height panel, width ≤ 900, scrolling
  without the horizontal. Esc / "Back" / a click outside — to the settings; a second Esc — everything closed.
- **The listing** — `tools/store_shots.py`: 5 subjects × 2 languages, 1920×1080, the phone layout, the Mobile renderer;
  `store/feature-graphic.png` (1024×500, the evening garden without the interface), `store/icon-512.png`, the thumbnail
  sheet `shots/store_contact.png`. New game flags: `--about`, `--nocoach` (no toasts or coach tips), `--noui`,
  `--zoom=`, `--midplay` (the most valuable item without which two stars stay lit goes back to the hand and is tried),
  `--nocard`; with `--shot` randomness is fixed. The floating "+N" from autosolve are removed from the frame.
- `store/.gdignore`, `store/*` in `exclude_filter`; `check_play`: `store/` is a leak, `versionName` ≠ `config/version`
  — FAIL, screenshots per language (`store/<lang>/screenshots/`, a shared fallback set).

**Found along the way.**
- The user, from a screenshot: in the rules card header the text padding on the left (6 px) was smaller than the
  vertical one (~14), and the header's rounding (16) wasn't concentric with the card (22 with a padding of 14). Now the
  padding is 14 and the radius 22 − 14 = 8; the header style was taken before the button entered the tree — from
  Godot's default theme (grey): now from the game theme. The geometry is locked in `test_touch` (the header sits exactly
  on the panel's padding, radius = R − padding, the left padding ≥ the vertical one).
- The layout test at 1333×750 (a 16:9 phone, the listing frame) passed without layout changes; the margin check now
  measures the bottom from the screen height, not from 600.

**Checks.** `test_settings` 118, `test_touch` 5,376 (layout ×3 sizes ×2 languages, About, the card header),
`test_i18n` 20,058 — 0 failures; the other tests are in the run log. `store_shots.py`: 10 shots + the graphic + the
icon, sizes verified, the player's files untouched (md5). `check_play --store`: the APK, the project, the graphics and
all 10 shots — OK; FAIL only on the listing texts (`title/short/full.txt` aren't written yet). `test_check_play`: every
planted violation is caught.

**Open.**

- The developer name for About and Play, the release version number (0.9 or 1.0) — the owner's decisions.
- The list of Android libraries — rescan after moving to Gradle (AAB).
- Accepting the listing: the owner reviews the thumbnail sheet.
